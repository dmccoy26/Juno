# Juno 0.2 workspace guide

November 18, 2026. This guide describes the current 0.2 source build. Consult BUILD-STATUS for installation and acceptance status.

## Find your work

The sidebar groups Today and Chat, your work tools, writing and recall, and saved sources. The sidebar button hides or shows navigation. Command-K opens **Search or go to**. Type a workspace name, a task title, room or other shared TaskDock field, or a memory phrase. Selecting a result opens its exact saved record. Search uses authorized saved copies; it does not refresh TaskDock's upstream sources. Ask Juno opens an unsent chat draft so you can review the question.

The source-status button lists dated saved copies and read failures. A successful read does not mean the original provider is current. Continue to use the connection workspace to refresh an approved source. An offline laptop shows its last complete copy and queued edit count.

## Review an issue and find time

Issues has a searchable result list, status filter and detail inspector. TaskDock facts and Juno planning details remain separate. In Planning details, choose one work arrangement: you, a worker independently, a worker with your help, or unconfirmed. Old contradictory records require your review. Funding and scheduled hours do not prove capability.

Save planning (Command-S) stores the changes directly in Juno. Cancel or Escape asks before discarding changes; an unchanged form closes immediately. A stale revision cannot silently replace someone else's update.

Find time carries the exact task, room, person, duration and supervision into Schedule. The room, person and possible-opening timelines share the same axis. Include your availability when comparing supervised work. On a narrow display, scroll the comparison horizontally; List & edit remains available for schedule maintenance.

Review hold offers a start within an actual opening. Saving rechecks availability, task requirements, weekly caps and existing holds. It creates a **Juno hold**, not a TaskDock assignment or a worker notification. Copy note copies a proposed time; you choose how to share it. Unknown meetings, holidays or closures remain coverage limits.

## Finish the day

Daily check-in asks five questions, one at a time. The progress overview names every step. A saved step opens its answer for review. Reminders from saved records are suggestions: Add to answer inserts only the item you select. Review it before saving. Command-Return saves and advances; your draft writing is retained while you work.

After the interview, review and confirm the diary. Only confirmed diaries are used in reporting. Correct the finished diary when wording needs to change; original interview notes do not override a corrected diary. Carry-forward details require their own planning review. Reminder permission and timing remain in Settings → Daily check-in.

## Review reports

Dean briefings are talking points with readable headings and lists. Presentation enlarges the document. Copy provides formatted and plain text for your destination application. Chair emails remain prose with the existing weekly routing: Alex/VISU includes GDES, VGAM and ANIM; Amy receives Sorin; Kathryn receives Performing Arts and Music. Holy Cross 203/326 remain Dean-only.

Each recipient has a reviewed status tied to the actual writing. Editing that email invalidates its reviewed mark. Reviewing another recipient does not review this one. Version history preserves earlier writing. In 0.2.8 and later, Choose evidence lets you include or omit individual routed facts, preview new wording, and explicitly save a new revision. Evidence changes require a fresh review; the original saved email is preserved. An explicit greeting update changes recognized greeting/signature lines while keeping the middle of the email intact. Unknown/custom formats are left for manual review.

Before Friday preparation, Juno shows the schedule rather than claiming a new draft exists. Saved historical drafts stay accessible. Refresh and comparison do not silently replace saved edits. Juno does not send these emails.

## Routines and purchasing

Recurring tasks currently stores **draft definitions** with upcoming-date previews. It supports daily, selected weekdays, monthly day and ordinal weekday rules, end dates and reviewed missed-run/overlap choices. Times use Central time; repeated DST times use the first occurrence and missing times advance to the first valid minute. No holiday calendar is assumed. **Task creation remains unavailable until TaskDock supplies the dedicated scoped write contract.** Saving a routine draft does not create a column or task.

Purchasing imports a reviewed Receipt Helper expense export. Preview the rows and observation date before importing. Amounts retain decimal precision and credits. Not checked, missing receipt and ambiguous matches remain different states. Juno excludes attachment paths and email contents. In 0.2.9 and later, Review task link connects an expense to the exact TaskDock workspace/task after your review. Links are references, not proof of funding, receipt or approval; changed expense facts or withdrawn task evidence require review again. Financial details are not automatically added to chair emails. Orders, report approvals and run history are not invented when the helper does not provide them. Submission, approval and payment remain in Concur.

## Private and Meeting conversations

Meeting mode uses a professional conversation and separate thread history. Private memories, diary recall, purchasing, worker reports and unclassified source views are hidden. Private memory search is unavailable. Old conversations remain private. Turning Meeting mode off restores private conversation navigation and drafts.

Meeting mode is a manual control; Juno does not claim to detect screen sharing. Reports and work issues remain visible for work discussions. Review the document you intend to present before sharing your screen.

## Updating and synchronizing

Your work lives in Application Support separately from the app bundle. Updates must pass backup, preservation and paired-device checks. Install a verified package on the Air; do not copy a development lab app. The work Mac is the host. The laptop can queue supported memory and diary edits offline and reconcile them when reconnected. It never runs a recurring TaskDock scheduler.


## Sources and recovery

Sources brings dated status, saved TaskDock tasks and saved evidence into one destination. Reloading saved status does not refresh an upstream service. Older Open TaskDock actions open its tab here.

Routines have retained rule history and recoverable retirement. Task creation remains unavailable until TaskDock provides its scoped write connection. Drafting a routine does not assign anyone or reserve a room.

Routine and chair-email edits can be recovered locally after reopening. Local recovery is not a saved report revision or a synchronized routine. Save explicitly to keep the reviewed version. If another computer has saved a newer email, compare it before choosing to keep recovered wording as a new edit. Discard removes the local recovery copy; existing saved versions remain.


## Today and where you stopped

Today puts the next question and current work preview before reference material. Wider windows place diary recall, remembered completions, local holds and report/source links alongside the work list; narrower windows stack them. The preview shows six matching issues, with search and a link to the full TaskDock copy.

Where you stopped reads the latest confirmed diary on or before today. Unconfirmed notes remain labeled separately. Corrected diary wording takes precedence over earlier interview answers. Read diary opens the full document. Pick up here prepares a chat message for you to review and send; it does not change tasks or planning facts. This private recall is hidden in Meeting mode. Offline recall is the dated copy from the last complete synchronization.


## Saved reports

Reports opens to Saved documents: a searchable list grouped by reporting week, with Dean talking points and each chair email named separately. Search a chair, subject or week; Command-F focuses that search. Load older weeks extends the list. Refresh keeps the loaded range so an older selected document stays available.

The document reader shows its actual week and revision. Copy, History and Mark reviewed are explicit actions; simply opening a document does not review it. Open editor keeps the selected week and recipient. Saved edits create a revision and return to the updated reader. Evidence & review notes remain separate from email prose. Preparation & schedules contains the automatic-run controls and preserves the document list when you return. Saved document browsing works from a complete offline copy; generation and edits that need the host remain unavailable offline.


## Source dates and overdue issues (0.2.21)

Today and Issues show **Last successful source updates** with Central dates and AM/PM times. A failed refresh keeps the earlier successful-copy time. **Reload status** reads the saved metadata; **Sources** opens the connection controls for an actual refresh. Provider update times may be older than Juno’s successful read time.

In Issues, choose **Overdue**, select a task, and choose **Add blocker…** or **Edit blocker…**. Record what is waiting, whom or what you need, the next step, and an optional follow-up date. **Save blocker** saves the reviewed detail directly in Juno. Other task-planning facts remain unchanged. Cancel lets you discard an unsaved edit. Clearing a local detail does not clear a blocker supplied by TaskDock. Reconnect before saving blockers from the laptop.


## Calendar, inline request answers and equipment links (0.2.22)

Today’s **Calendar commitments** card is separate from **Local planning holds**. It shows saved calendar events overlapping today in Central time, including all-day events, and provides coverage dates. Missing or stale copies do not establish free time. Meeting mode hides event titles. **Reload agenda** reads the saved copy; refresh the original connection in Settings when necessary. See [Google Calendar setup](GOOGLE-CALENDAR-SETUP.md).

Issues now lets you type an answer below **Next detail to clarify** and choose **Save answer**. An explicit **None / not applicable** choice is separate from an unknown answer. Your reviewed Juno fact and date appear in request details. TaskDock is unchanged. Changed source facts require another review, and an unsubmitted answer is retained on this Mac independently of canonical saves. These answers need the connected work Mac; offline memory and diary support remains unchanged.

In Purchasing, select an expense and choose **Review equipment link…**. Search equipment names, room or exact stable IDs and explicitly review the selected equipment. The picker only covers authorized equipment referenced in the current Beacon feed. Changed or stale evidence makes the reference need review. Removing a reference keeps the equipment, expense and review history. Financial and repair states are not inferred from a link.


### Work calendar automatic refresh (0.2.24)

In Settings → Connections → Direct programs → Google Calendar, discover your account, select calendars, review your privacy/coverage choices and enable automatic refresh. Close Settings. The work Mac refreshes every 15 minutes while Juno is open and awake, keeping seven days back and 90 days ahead in Central time. Today shows status and successful-copy dates. Turning it off retains the last copy. Failed reads retain evidence and show retry/review guidance. Your Air uses the work Mac’s shared dated copy and never receives OAuth credentials. See [calendar setup](GOOGLE-CALENDAR-SETUP.md).


## Starting and returning to work — 0.2.27

Today begins with **One small next step**. Choose **Help me get started**, name the work, enter a first action and a clear finish point, then choose **Start this**. You can select a saved issue to reuse its reviewed planning notes, or begin with any piece of work. **Use a tiny starting step** supplies an editable opening action without needing LM Studio. This saves a personal step; it does not start a timer, assign work or change TaskDock.

When interrupted, choose **I was interrupted**. Your action and finish point stay saved immediately. **Add a stopping note** records a precise place to return to. **Resume this step** restores the saved action. **Help me restart** offers making the step smaller, naming a blocker, or deliberately parking the current work and choosing different work. Parked steps remain under **Saved for later**. Clear a resolved personal blocker in the bookmark editor.

**Step finished** records that small step, without closing the TaskDock issue or confirming a repair. The most recent finished step is shown on Today. These private bookmarks do not automatically enter a diary, chair email or Dean report. Meeting mode hides them.

A connected paired Mac can update the same bookmark. If another screen or Mac saves first, Juno keeps your typed draft and asks you to compare every saved detail before applying it. Cancel can keep a local draft for later or discard only that draft. Offline copies display the saved bookmark; bookmark editing requires reconnection in this increment. Existing memory/diary offline queues and laptop pairing are unchanged.
